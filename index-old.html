About Me

I am an accounting and finance professional with a Bachelor's degree in Accounting from Civil Aviation University of China, and I'm about to pursue my MSc in Accounting and Financial Management at Uppsala University in Sweden.

During my nearly two years of overseas work in Uganda, I independently managed end-to-end financial operations for three real estate projects — from fund allocation and foreign exchange to budget control and financial reporting — building strong adaptability and cross-cultural communication skills.
